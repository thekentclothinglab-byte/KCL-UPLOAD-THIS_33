01 What we do

Our Services

Four specialist processes, all in-house. Each one mastered. Pick one or combine them for your project.

001
🧵

Embroidery

Thread stitched directly into the fabric with precision. Durable, professional, and perfect for logos and brand marks that need to last wash after wash.

Caps Polo shirts Workwear Hoodies
002
🔥

Heat Press

High-pressure transfers applied with precision heat. Bold, vivid colour and clean edges — ideal for streetwear drops, events, and brands that need volume fast.

Vinyl T-shirts Hoodies Joggers
003
🎨

Sublimation

Dye bonded into the fabric at a molecular level. Photographic quality, all-over coverage. Zero cracking, zero fading — ever.

All-over print Sportswear Lifestyle Photographic
004
🖨️

DTF Printing

Direct to Film transfers printed in full colour and applied to virtually any fabric. No minimums, no weeding, incredibly detailed results on dark or light garments.

Full colour Dark fabrics No minimums Any garment
